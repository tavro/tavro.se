    Opera Software

    we took part in Stadsvandringen, the walk the university arranges so the new students get shown around town and hear from the companies here, with Opera as one of the stops. i helped plan our part of it and was there on the day. the summer interns have finished their projects, which means i am back working on the client again. most recently i have been integrating WalletConnect, an open protocol that lets a crypto wallet on your phone talk to a website or an app without the wallet ever handing over its keys. i have also been "benchmarking" AI models to work out which ones are actually the most cost effective for what we need them to do.
